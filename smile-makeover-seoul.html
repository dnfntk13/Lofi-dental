What does a smile makeover involve?

Smile makeover is a way to describe an individual treatment plan, rather than one procedure. Your goals might include a more consistent tooth shade, a different tooth shape or a smile that feels more balanced. The useful first question is what you want to change and why it matters to you.

A cosmetic dentistry consultation should leave you with a clear understanding of the options for your own teeth. Bring questions about the features you would like to keep as well as those you want to change. A photograph can help explain a preference, but another person's result is not a prediction of yours.

Whitening, veneers or restorative dentistry?

Teeth whitening addresses the shade of natural teeth. Dental veneers cover the front surfaces and may address shape as well as appearance. When missing or damaged teeth are part of your concern, the conversation may also include dental implants or a broader restorative treatment plan.

Ask how the suggested approach relates to your main concern, which teeth it would involve and whether alternatives are available. There is no fixed number of veneers or identical treatment sequence for every smile makeover.

Your English consultation in Apgujeong

Explain your goals in your own words: brighter teeth, a chipped edge, uneven proportions or concerns about previous dental work. Share your dental history and any problems with comfort or chewing. The examination helps put those concerns into context before treatment is selected.

Useful questions include: What changes are realistic for my teeth? What preparation is involved? What maintenance will I need? How will we decide on shade and shape? You can also ask to discuss options in stages before choosing a plan.

Planning a smile makeover during a Seoul visit

If you are comparing cosmetic dental care in Seoul while arranging a trip to Korea, share your arrival and departure dates early. Ask which appointments are for assessment, treatment or follow-up, and what should happen if more time is needed.
