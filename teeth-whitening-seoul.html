Is teeth whitening suitable for your smile?

Tooth discoloration has different causes, and whitening does not work equally well for every situation. An examination helps determine whether whitening is an appropriate option. Tell the dentist when you noticed the color change and whether you have tried whitening before.

When looking for teeth whitening in Seoul, prepare questions about your own teeth rather than choosing a result from a photograph. Ask what kind of improvement can reasonably be discussed after your examination and what factors might limit it.

Whitening with crowns, veneers or fillings

Whitening changes the color of natural teeth, but it does not lighten crowns, veneers or fillings. Existing restorations may therefore look different from the surrounding teeth after whitening.

If you are also considering veneers or a smile makeover, discuss shade matching and the order of treatment before making a decision. Mention dental work in the visible part of your smile, even if it was completed many years ago.

Sensitivity and aftercare

Some people experience tooth sensitivity or gum irritation with whitening. Tell the dentist about existing sensitivity before treatment and ask what to do if discomfort occurs. Your aftercare instructions should reflect the treatment you receive.

Ask which products to use, how to follow the instructions and when to contact the clinic. Avoid combining whitening products on your own in the hope of a faster result. Discuss ongoing care and any future whitening with your dentist.

Your whitening consultation in Apgujeong

Bring your priorities: a brighter natural shade, questions about a darker tooth or concerns about matching previous dental work. You can discuss these in English at our clinic in Gangnam, Seoul.

Questions worth asking include: Is whitening appropriate for me? What happens to the color of my existing restorations? How should I plan around sensitivity? What follow-up would be helpful? A consultation gives you room to understand those answers before proceeding.

Planning a dental visit during your stay in Seoul

Share your travel dates and any events you are planning around. Ask the clinic about appointment timing after your needs have been assessed. Results and visit requirements vary, so an appointment request is the start of a discussion rather than a guaranteed treatment schedule.
