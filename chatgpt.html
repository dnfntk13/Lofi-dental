Lofi's public information tools let you compare our published dental fee guides, understand general treatment concepts and find practical visiting information. English is the default language.

What you can explore

  • Fees with and without Korean National Health Insurance, using our current price guide.
  • Published explanations of smile makeover, veneers, implants, full mouth restoration and whitening.
  • Our address, regular opening hours and contact options.

These tools provide general information. They do not determine your diagnosis, treatment suitability, insurance eligibility or final quote. They cannot access patient records, make or change appointments, or send messages. Please do not submit personal details, symptoms, medical records or photographs to these tools.

Connection for testing
