Questions about dental prices

Does “With KNHI” mean all treatments are covered by insurance?

No. “With KNHI” means our clinic’s prices for patients with active Korean National Health Insurance. It does not mean every treatment in the table is insured. Extraction and checkup & cleaning estimates in that column apply to eligible covered care.

How much is a dental implant at lofi in Seoul?

Our dental implant price guide is ₩700,000–900,000 per tooth for patients with active KNHI and ₩1,000,000–1,500,000 per tooth without KNHI. We confirm the included treatment, any additional procedures, applicable taxes and your final cost before you proceed.

How much do veneers cost at lofi in Seoul?

Our veneer price guide is ₩200,000–450,000 per tooth for patients with active KNHI and ₩400,000–700,000 per tooth without KNHI. Your treatment needs determine the final plan and cost.

How much is a checkup and cleaning with or without Korean insurance?

Our checkup & cleaning price guide is ₩20,000–30,000 for eligible covered care with active KNHI and ₩100,000 without KNHI. The clinic confirms your eligibility, included care and final cost before treatment.

Are taxes and additional procedures included in these prices?

The table is a price guide. We confirm the included treatment, any additional procedures, applicable taxes and your final cost before you proceed. Ask our team to confirm the scope of your treatment and, for whitening, the number of cycles in your plan.
