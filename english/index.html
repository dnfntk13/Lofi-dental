A guide to your smile.
Dental care in Seoul.

Smile makeover, veneers, dental implants, full mouth restoration and teeth whitening at lofi esthetic dentistry in Apgujeong, Gangnam. Explore how each treatment addresses a different concern, with English consultation to help you understand your options.

01Smile makeover

A smile makeover brings several treatment options into one individual plan. It starts with what you would like to change: tooth color, shape, proportions or the overall balance of your smile. Depending on your teeth and goals, the plan may involve whitening, veneers or restorative treatment.

At your consultation, we discuss your priorities alongside the condition of your teeth, gums and bite. The aim is to plan the appearance of your smile together with its everyday function, rather than choose the same treatment for every tooth.

Find out more →

02Veneers

Dental veneers are custom coverings placed on the front surfaces of teeth. They can address concerns such as tooth shape, visible chips, gaps or discoloration. Planning considers the shade, length and proportions of the teeth you show when you smile.

Veneers and crowns cover different parts of a tooth. Your examination helps determine which approach is appropriate and how much preparation would be needed. Discuss the long-term commitment before deciding on treatment.

Find out more →

03Dental implants

Dental implants support replacement teeth when one or more natural teeth are missing. An implant can support a crown, while a plan for several missing teeth may use an implant-supported bridge or denture.

Planning begins with an assessment of your oral health, available bone and medical history. Placement, healing and the final restoration may take place over separate visits. If you are traveling to Seoul, discuss your travel dates and follow-up arrangements before committing to an implant treatment schedule.

Find out more →

04Full mouth restoration

Full mouth restoration considers the teeth and bite as a whole when several areas need care. For someone with multiple missing, damaged or worn teeth, the discussion goes beyond the appearance of a single tooth to include chewing, support and how the teeth meet.

A plan may combine different restorations and, where appropriate, implants. Your consultation establishes the priorities and sequence of care, including which existing teeth can be maintained and how follow-up fits into your schedule.

Find out more →

05Teeth whitening

Teeth whitening focuses on the color of natural teeth. It may be an option when your main concern is staining or a darker shade rather than tooth shape. An examination helps identify the cause of discoloration and whether whitening is suitable.

Whitening does not change the color of existing crowns, veneers or fillings. If you have these restorations, discuss shade matching as part of your plan. We also discuss sensitivity, expected changes and aftercare before treatment.

Find out more →
