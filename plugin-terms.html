Lofi public information tools

Public information tool terms

Effective October 29, 2026

These tools provide lofi esthetic dentistry's published clinic information, fee guides and general treatment overviews. They do not provide an individual diagnosis, a treatment recommendation, emergency care, an insurance eligibility decision or a binding quotation. An examination and discussion with the clinic are needed to establish an individual treatment plan.

Fees are in Korean won. Clinic prices for patients enrolled in Korean National Health Insurance do not mean every listed treatment is insured. The clinic confirms eligibility, included treatment, any additional procedures, applicable taxes and the final cost before treatment. Regular hours do not confirm holiday opening or appointment availability.

The tools do not create, change or cancel appointments, send messages, take payments or access patient records. Following a contact-information link does not itself confirm a booking. Please do not send personal, medical or payment information as tool arguments.

Tool results include public source links so you can check the published information. Information can change; contact the clinic to confirm details relevant to your visit. Use the tools for their stated public information purpose and do not attempt unauthorized access to other clinic systems.

For questions, contact lofidentalcs@lofiesthetic.com. See also plugin privacy.
